## The flywheel

Every trade your token does comes back as $CLAW and $ANSEM.

1. **You trade** — your token launches on ClawPump and the market does what it does.
2. **Fees accrue** — ClawPump keeps 35% of trading fees. **The rest is yours, win or lose.**
3. **Buys go on the book** — 10% of ClawPump's share buys $CLAW and $ANSEM on the open market, 50/50.
4. **Builders get paid** — those buys fund further participant rewards, bringing more teams and more volume.

The 10% comes out of ClawPump's 35% share, **not** your side of the fees. Applies to teams deploying on clawpump.tech; EasyA Kickstart entries sit outside the loop. Live numbers: https://clawpump.tech/analytics
